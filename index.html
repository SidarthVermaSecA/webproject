Account Types

Checking Account

Provides easy access to your money through checks, debit cards, and ATMs. Often used for daily transactions like bill payments and purchases. Offers interest on the balance but usually requires a higher minimum balance and may have monthly fees.

Saving Account

Standard Checking Account: Provides easy access to your money through checks, debit cards, and ATMs. Often used for daily transactions like bill payments and purchases. Interest-Bearing Checking Account: Offers interest on the balance but usually requires a higher minimum balance and may have monthly fees.

Money Marketing Account

Combines features of checking and savings accounts. Offers higher interest rates than savings accounts but often requires a higher minimum balance.
